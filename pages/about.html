MY STORY

A passion for the natural world.

Based on the North West coast of Scotland, Malcolm Lismore is a freelance photographer with a deep passion for the natural world and the stories found within it.

From rugged Highland landscapes and dramatic coastlines to Scotland's wildlife and coastal birds, photography provides a way to preserve the atmosphere and character of the places that inspire him.

While the natural world remains at the heart of his photography, Malcolm also works with people, photographing weddings, portraits and special occasions.

THE APPROACH

More than a photograph.

Every photograph should communicate something — the atmosphere of a landscape, the character of wildlife or the emotion of a moment shared with people.

01

Observe

Taking time to understand the environment, light and atmosphere before pressing the shutter.

02

Capture

Creating photographs that feel natural, authentic and connected to the moment being experienced.

03

Preserve

Turning fleeting moments into photographs that can be revisited and remembered for years to come.

THE NATURAL WORLD

Inspired by wild Scotland.

Scotland's landscapes provide a constantly changing subject. Weather, seasons, wildlife and light can transform the same location from one day to the next.

This connection with the natural environment is a major influence on Malcolm's work and continues to shape the way he approaches photography.
